### Image Generation Prompt

Central visual: a technical exploded-diagram of an interlocking gear-and-worm-drive assembly rendered in precise Leonardo-style sepia ink — proportional ratio lines, marginal arithmetic annotations, force-vector arrows. Left margin: faint Vitruvian geometry circle and square ghost-layers in charcoal wash. A central shaft runs vertically through the composition, all subsidiary systems visibly feeding it. Top third: a restrained horizontal gold title panel with slight embossed depth. The machine has weight and permanence — mathematical certainty made physical. 4:6 portrait. Print-ready 300dpi.
